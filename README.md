# 📊 Customer Complaint & Service Analytics Dashboard

An interactive customer complaint analytics dashboard developed using **Microsoft Excel and Power Query** to analyze complaint trends, resolution performance, SLA compliance, customer satisfaction, and support channels.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Customer service teams receive complaints across multiple channels and issue categories, making it difficult to monitor resolution performance, SLA compliance, customer satisfaction, and recurring complaint patterns.

The objective of this project is to transform raw customer complaint data using **Power Query** and develop an interactive Excel dashboard that helps customer service teams:

- Monitor complaint volume and trends.
- Track complaint resolution performance.
- Measure SLA compliance.
- Identify high-volume complaint categories.
- Analyze resolution time by complaint type.
- Compare customer satisfaction across complaint categories.
- Evaluate complaint performance across support channels.

## 📈 Key KPIs

- Total Complaints: **1,200**
- Average Resolution Time: **27.7 Hours**
- Resolution Rate: **65.3%**
- Average Customer Rating: **3.2 / 5**
- Resolved Complaints: **783**
- In Progress Complaints: **241**
- Pending Complaints: **176**
- SLA Compliance: **57.0%**

## 🔎 Key Insights

1. **Resolution performance** – The overall resolution rate is **65.3%**, indicating that a significant proportion of complaints require further action.

2. **SLA compliance** – SLA compliance is **57.0%**, highlighting an opportunity to improve response and resolution processes.

3. **High resolution time** – Fraud Investigation complaints have the highest average resolution time at more than **60 hours**, making them a key area for operational attention.

4. **Complaint volume** – Fraud Investigation and Card Problem are among the highest-volume complaint categories, indicating areas where recurring issues should be investigated to reduce complaint volume.
5. **Channel performance** – Complaint volumes and resolution outcomes vary across support channels, providing opportunities to optimize resource allocation.

6. **Customer satisfaction** – Customer ratings differ across complaint categories, allowing service teams to identify areas where the customer experience can be improved.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Improve SLA compliance** by identifying complaint categories with consistently long resolution times.

2. **Prioritize high-volume complaint categories** and investigate recurring issues to reduce future complaint volumes.

3. **Create specialized workflows for complex complaints**, particularly Fraud Investigation cases, to reduce resolution time and improve SLA performance.

4. **Complaint volume** – Fraud Investigation and Card Problem are among the highest-volume complaint categories, indicating areas where recurring issues should be investigated to reduce complaint volume.

5. **Investigate low-rated complaint categories** to understand the factors affecting customer satisfaction.

6. **Optimize support channels** by comparing complaint volume, resolution rate, and SLA performance.

7. **Use Power Query for repeatable data preparation** so that the dashboard can be refreshed efficiently when new complaint data becomes available.
